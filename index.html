📜 Royal Tambola — Rules & How to Play

🌐 Any Network • Any Area Connection

  • Cross-Network Play: Works across 4G/5G mobile data, home Wi-Fi, and different cities/countries via global STUN/TURN relays.
  • Host a Game: Enter your name and tap Host New Room to generate a unique Room ID (TAM-XXXX) or share the direct invite link.
  • Join a Game: Friends in any area enter the Room ID (or open the shared link) and tap Join Room to receive their unique 15-number ticket.

🎮 How to Play

  1. The Host draws numbers from 1 to 90 manually or via Auto Draw (5s).
  2. When a called number matches your 3×9 ticket, tap it to cross it off with a hand-drawn pen mark.
  3. As soon as you complete a winning pattern, tap the matching Prize Sticker button before other players!
  4. Anti-Bogey Protection: Uncalled numbers cannot be marked, and every claim is automatically verified against the Host's called numbers.

🏆 Winning Prize Patterns

⚡ Jaldi FiveFirst player to cross off any 5 numbers on their ticket.
⬆️ Top LineAll 5 numbers in the top row of the ticket.
➡️ Middle LineAll 5 numbers in the middle row of the ticket.
⬇️ Bottom LineAll 5 numbers in the bottom row of the ticket.
📐 Four CornersFirst & last numbers of both the Top Row and Bottom Row (4 numbers).
👑 Full HouseAll 15 numbers on the entire ticket crossed off.
