📖 About Digital Tambola (Housie) & Complete Guide

🎟️ Unique 15-Number Tickets

Each Tambola (Housie) ticket is a 3×9 grid containing 15 unique numbers from 1 to 90 arranged across nine columns. Players can shuffle and pick a ticket before a round starts and mark called numbers as the host draws.

🏆 Standard Prize Patterns

Players can claim Jaldi Five (first 5 called numbers), Top Line, Middle Line, Bottom Line, Four Corners (first and last numbers of top and bottom rows), and Full House (all 15 numbers).

🌐 Multiplayer Rooms & Languages

Host a room to get a Room ID (such as VSA-XXXX), invite friends via link or WhatsApp, draw numbers manually or with Auto Draw, and switch between English, Telugu (తెలుగు), and Hindi (हिंदी).

🛡️ Privacy & Fair Play

Room state and ticket selections are managed during live sessions with browser storage used only for local preferences (language, audio, and popup settings). Published by Digital Swami.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is Digital Tambola (Housie)?

Digital Tambola (also known as Housie or Indian Bingo) is a free browser-based multiplayer number game where a host draws numbers from 1 to 90 and players mark matching numbers on 3×9 tickets containing 15 unique numbers.

Are all Tambola tickets unique for every player?

Yes. The host tracks issued ticket signatures so every player in the room receives a strictly distinct 15-number ticket with no duplicate number sets.

What are the winning patterns in Digital Tambola?

Players can claim six prize patterns: Jaldi Five (any 5 numbers), Top Line, Middle Line, Bottom Line, Four Corners (first and last numbers of top and bottom rows), and Full House (all 15 numbers).

Which languages are supported?

Digital Tambola supports English, Telugu (తెలుగు), and Hindi (हिंदी) for both the interface and the built-in voice number caller.

Do players need to install an app to play?

No app download or account registration is required. Players can join directly in any modern mobile or desktop browser using the Room ID or WhatsApp invite link.
