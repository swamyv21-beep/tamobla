📜 Digital Tambola — Rules & How to Play

🎟️ 100% Unique Tickets • 2 to 100 Players

  • No Same Numbers / No Duplicate Tickets: The Host generates and assigns a strictly unique 15-number ticket to every player—no two players ever get the same ticket numbers.
  • Room Capacity (2–100 Players): Set room size from 2 up to 100 players across 4G/5G mobile data, Wi-Fi, and any city.
  • Join a Game: Friends enter the Room ID (VSA-XXXX) or open the shared link to receive their unique assigned ticket.

🎮 How to Play

  1. The Host draws numbers from 1 to 90 manually or via Auto Draw (5s).
  2. When a called number matches your 3×9 ticket, tap it to cross it off with a hand-drawn pen mark.
  3. As soon as you complete a winning pattern, tap the matching Prize Sticker button before other players!
  4. Anti-Bogey Protection: Uncalled numbers cannot be marked, and every claim is verified against the Host's issued ticket.
