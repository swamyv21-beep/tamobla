📖 About Digital Tambola (Housie) & Complete Guide

🎟️ Unique 15-Number Tickets

Each Tambola (Housie) ticket is a 3×9 grid containing 15 unique numbers from 1 to 90 arranged across nine columns. Play 1, 2, or 3 tickets per round and mark called numbers as the host draws.

🏆 Standard & Bonus Prize Patterns

Hosts can enable Jaldi Five, Early Seven, Top/Middle/Bottom Lines, Four Corners, Breakfast/Lunch/Dinner columns, and Full House.

🌐 Multiplayer Rooms & Languages

Host a room to get a Room ID (such as VSA-XXXX), share via QR code or WhatsApp, react live with emojis, and switch between English, Telugu (తెలుగు), and Hindi (हिंदी).

🛡️ Privacy & Fair Play

Room state and ticket selections are managed during live sessions with browser storage used only for local preferences. Published by Digital Swami.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is Digital Tambola (Housie)?

Digital Tambola (also known as Housie or Indian Bingo) is a free browser-based multiplayer number game where a host draws numbers from 1 to 90 and players mark matching numbers on 3×9 tickets containing 15 unique numbers.

Are all Tambola tickets unique for every player?

Yes. The host tracks issued ticket signatures so every player in the room receives strictly distinct 15-number tickets with no duplicate number sets.

What are the winning patterns in Digital Tambola?

Players can claim Jaldi Five, Early Seven, Top Line, Middle Line, Bottom Line, Four Corners, Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, and Full House.

Which languages are supported?

Digital Tambola supports English, Telugu (తెలుగు), and Hindi (हिंदी) for both the interface and the built-in voice number caller.
